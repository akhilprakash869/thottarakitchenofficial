The Story of
Thottara Kitchen

Nestled near Olippuram, Arayankavu in Ernakulam, Thottara Kitchen is not just a destination—it is an experience. Born out of a deep passion for authentic Kerala cuisine and unparalleled hospitality, we have curated a space where tradition meets modern elegance.

Our chefs use only the freshest locally-sourced ingredients to craft dishes that tell an unforgettable story with every bite. Join us for a journey of taste, comfort, and luxury.
